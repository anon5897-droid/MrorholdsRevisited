Castle NPCs:

- [ ] _Laren Bakker-_ A short, melancholy ==**human**== member of the Sharn Watch. He uses as few words as possible, and distrusts women, as his mother left him and his wife is cheating on him. His dream was to become a leatherworker, and you can open him up with a fine piece of leather armor or talking about that in general. (Guard stats)

- [ ] _Riki d’Sivis-_ A well-respected **==gnome==** magewright with a Mark of Scribing. She manages several sending stations located throughout the Middle and Upper Districts. She has a “work hard, play hard” mentality, and loves to prank people with her tarantula pet. (Magewright stats)

- [ ] _Dover Carch-_ A **==human==** transplant to Sharn from Aundair, he has a small wand store located in Clifftop. He makes some of the most durable wands in Sharn, making him a favorite of adventurers. However, he has a not so secret drinking problem and a disdain for gnomes. He occasionally takes potshots at them with a wand of polymorph when he’s drunk. (Mage stats)

- [ ] _Fromm Altaner-_ A **==changeling==** rogue who turned into a halfling for a job a few weeks back, got a concussion in the process, and now cannot remember how to change into anything else. She is relentlessly good-natured and optimistic, although she admits being stuck in one form is affecting her livelihood. (Assassin stats)

- [ ] _Ivelo Toppe-_ A filthy ==**half-elf**== beggar who keeps getting kicked out of Skysedge Park. He says that he goes there in order to connect to nature and to make offerings to the fey of Thelanis, for a transgression that his ancestor had made long ago. (Not honoring a warlock pact.) He believes that archfey will knock Sharn out of the sky if he doesn’t continue. (Commoner stats)

- [ ] _Minyu Jonz-_ A mountain of a **==human==**, he serves with the Redcloaks. He has a very firm and unwavering sense of honor, he is constantly trying to hide an aberrant dragonmark and the accidents that happen because of it. He is being heavily recruited by the House Tarkanan for a plot. (Knight stats)

- [ ] _Zero-_ A ==**warforged**== who got the name because she leaves no stone unturned, Zero is an intrepid reporter for the Breland Chronicle. However, her skill with writing does not translate well to making friends, and she desperately wants someone to hang out with. (Warforged stats)

- [ ] _Xanaphia Siannodel-_ An **==elven==** warrior who has traveled to every continent in Eberron, as well as Thelanis and Syrania, she is finally looking to retire. However, she is persistent and doesn’t know how to relax. She has taken up aggressively categorizing every flower in Breland in order to write a book, start a shop, or become a botanist. She isn’t sure yet. (Archmage stats)

- [ ] _Curlot Wrighten-_ A bartender ==**???**== of _Inn Harm’s Way_ in Middle Menthis, Curlot has a different answer for every single person who asks why his arm is missing. He will also speak enthusiastically and at length about the benefits of meditation. Look away for too long, however, and you may find your pockets a bit lighter. (Bandit stat block.)

- [ ] _Taris Irvallo-_ A beautiful **==human==** from Thrane, she left her country to make her way as a singer. She currently works as a cocktail waitress at the Grey Dragon Inn, and does the occasional performance in Lower Menthis. She is constantly on the lookout for new auditions, and she still attends weekly services for the Silver Flame as she promised her mother that she would not let the big city corrupt her. However, she usually goes to these services hungover. (Commoner stats)

- [ ] _Barimer Brand-_ A ==**shifter**== blacksmith who is motivated by greed. He will promise anything to anyone, as long as they are willing to pay. He has a goblin employee/indentured servant. He loves women in distress, and fancies himself a knight in shining armor. His shop name is Barimer’s Blacksmithy. (Bandit Captain stats)

- [ ] _Inazxia Wrighten-_ A **==tiefling==** gambler who works for the Boromar Clan. She is cocky and streetsmart. She doesn’t follow any religion, because they don’t look out for people like her. She spends a lot of time as far up in the city she can go, staring at the stars after a long shift.

- [ ] _Sipho-_ A **==goblin==** mercenary for Daask. Perpetually complaining, he loves finding bugs and other creepy crawlies in the Lower Wards. His apartment is near full of them. No one likes to come over. (Bard stats)

- [ ] _Aeliss Sanne-_ A high-ranking member of the Blood of Vol, he is a **==vampire==**. He usually attempts to keep the general public from knowing about this, however. He loves making up elaborate lies and fake rites about the religion for outsiders, so he can get away with doing whatever he wants. He is actually quite devout, however, and cares for his people deeply. (Vampire stats)

- [ ] _Junior-_ A 7’ tall massive **==warforged==**, he got the name ironically. He works as a bouncer for the Red Hammer, and is suspicious of any non-warforged due to the discrimination he’s faced. Once you show him some decency, he softens up and enjoys telling you about his hobby of sewing cloaks.

- [ ] _Etcher-_ An entirely purple **==warforged==**, he works for the Keldoran forge, learning from the greats. He has begun to learn to enjoy his now ample free time, and knows everything about everyone in the Cogs. He has a pet cat named Scratches with half his fur burned off.

- [ ] _Book-_ True to his word, this generic soldier **==warforged==** has his nose stuck in a book. He’s reading _Kobolds in my Closet._ It’s a humorous account of a human who found kobolds trying to take over his house and his attempts to ward them off. He is not entirely talkative, and a close friend of Etcher.

- [ ] _Vi-_ a **==Warforged==**, named for serial number 6 on his cheek, he's the oldest warforged anyone has kept track of. Vi is incredibly beat up, with marks all over his body from the war, but also up beat and quick with any number of stories he has from the war, although he's prone to repeating a few lines over. Talking with him for too long will bring on memories of his allies who didn't survive the war though

- [ ] _Zed and Amelia d'Tharashk_- a pair of ==**half-orc**== siblings and bounty hunters of house Tharashk, although only Amelia has the dragonmark. Amelia is pretty introverted and notoriously bad at talking to people but an amazing hunter, therefore she lets her friendly, outgoing, and handsome brother Zed do the talking for the two of them, while she focuses on the hunting work.

- [ ] _J'kel-_ **==Kobold==** architect and minor leader of Droaam, sent out on a humanitarian/diplomatic mission to the five nations in order to try and curry favor for the new nation. They go out to try and help communities that were effected by the war and repair destroyed buildings. Followed by a kobold retinue, and a few gnoll guards.

- [ ] _Ella Moonsong_- Longtooth wolf ==**Shifter**== mercenary, found wherever a person has a job that needs doing. Quiet and cool headed in a crisis, she has only one thing that sets her off: the church of the silver flame. Stories from her family, caught up in the Lycanthropic Purge, has fostered a disdain for the church and Thrane within her.

- [ ] _Godfrey and Celsus-_ Two young men from what used to be Cyre, they have decided to go out and try to make their way in the world. Godfrey is a giant of a ==**half-elf**==-who is strong but an easily embarrassed person, and soft spoken despite his large size. Celsus is a **==human==**, small and quick to speak, and is the one coming up with their harebrained schemes, but is actually quite smart if you get to know him.

- [ ] _Lucan d'Thuranni_- An aspiring **==Thuranni==** assassin, currently working undercover to keep up the appearances of the Thuranni. His branch of the family is close to the baron, and hoped that would get him into world of assassination-what he sees as actual Thuranni work. He'll keep up appearances-entertaining and delighting-but getting to know him shows he is frustrated by where he is, and hoping to move beyond.
