## Encounter: Dolgrim Scavenger Ambush

As the party traverses a narrow mountain pass, they discover the remains of a dwarven merchant caravan. The wagons have been ripped apart from below rather than attacked from above. Strange clicking noises echo from cracks in the rock.

Moments later, twisted goblinoid creatures emerge.

### Monsters

- 8 × Dolgrims (CR 1/2 each)
- 2 × Dolgaunts (CR 3 each)

The monsters attempt to:

- Hide among rocks until the party investigates.
- Use the dolgrims to pin down the front line.
- Have the dolgaunts use their blindsight and reach to attack spellcasters from behind cover.

This is roughly a **Hard** encounter for six 5th-level characters according to the 2014 DMG encounter-building guidelines.

### Terrain

- Narrow mountain trail.
- Steep drop on one side.
- Loose scree (Dexterity save DC 13 or fall prone when dashing).
- A collapsed mine entrance that can be explored afterward.

### Treasure

Inside the abandoned mine:

- 300 gp in raw mithral ore.
- A damaged dwarven journal describing increasing "voices from below."
- A dragonshard worth 100 gp.
- Evidence that the creatures came from deeper tunnels, foreshadowing future adventures.
